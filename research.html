Research

Research

I study how teams of aerial robots can coordinate under limited communication and partial observability, from decentralized guidance and task allocation to learned multi-agent policies, and how to take these methods from simulation to real flights.

Topics

Decentralized multi-UAV guidance

Swarm-inspired (PSO-based) guidance laws for fleets that must track and monitor evolving phenomena such as wildfire fronts.

Multi-agent reinforcement learning

MAPPO / HAPPO for heterogeneous fleets, with recurrent and spatio-temporal (ConvLSTM) representations of the environment.

Multi-robot task allocation

Fault-tolerant and consensus-based allocation, search & rescue and exploration under limited communication.

Onboard perception & field trials

Embedded fire and smoke detection (YOLO) and hardware-in-the-loop to outdoor validation of multi-UAV systems.

Projects

ANR FireFlies

Multi-UAV wildfire monitoring with ENAC and LAAS-CNRS (ANR-23-IAS2-0002-01): decentralized guidance, MARL for heterogeneous fleets and onboard fire perception.
