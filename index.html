About

I am a faculty member at ENSTA (Institut Polytechnique de Paris), in the U2IS lab, where I work on coordination and decision-making for teams of aerial robots. My research combines decentralized guidance, multi-robot task allocation and multi-agent reinforcement learning, with a strong emphasis on validating methods on real drones in the field.

Before joining ENSTA, I was a postdoctoral researcher at ENAC (École Nationale de l'Aviation Civile) in Toulouse, in the SysDyn team, working on the ANR FireFlies project with LAAS-CNRS on multi-UAV wildfire monitoring. I received my PhD from Normandie University (GREAH lab, Le Havre) in December 2024, with the European label, on multi-robot task allocation with gossip-based consensus, validated on quadrotor platforms. During my PhD I was a visiting researcher at the University of Cagliari. I hold an MSc from the University of Montpellier.
